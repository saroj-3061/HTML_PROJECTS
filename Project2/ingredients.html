🛒Ingredients You Need for 🍝 Creamy White Sauce Pasta


For Pasta

  • 200 g pasta (penne, fusilli, or macaroni)
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil

For the White Sauce

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 2 cups milk (500 ml)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon oregano
  • ½ teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
  • ½ cup grated mozzarella or cheddar cheese

For Vegetables(Optional)

  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped capsicum
  • ½ cup sweet corn
  • ½ cup mushrooms
  • ½ cup broccoli
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
