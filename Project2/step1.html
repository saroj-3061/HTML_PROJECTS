Step 1: Boil the pasta

Steps to Follow:

  1. Pour 2 liters of water into a large pot.
  2. Add 1 teaspoon salt.
  3. Add 1 teaspoon oil.
  4. Bring the water to a rolling boil.
  5. Add the pasta.
  6. Cook for 8–10 minutes or according to the package instructions.
  7. Stir occasionally so it doesn't stick.
  8. Drain the pasta in a strainer.
  9. Rinse briefly with cool water.
  10. Set aside.
